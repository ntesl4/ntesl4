I run **Linesoft**, a studio building **martech and adtech** products: tools that help brands and agencies understand their ads, outsmart competitors and turn conversations into revenue.

Most of our work lives in private client repositories. Recent builds:

**Adtech**
- Meta Ads competitor intelligence: daily scans of rival ads, launch/stop detection, creative archive, Slack digests
- Ad performance reporting platform for agencies and e-commerce brands
- AI ad creative review for static and video ads, scored against real campaign metrics

**Martech**
- AI funnel audit for e-commerce: landing pages, GA4 data and organic content, with actionable fixes
- Content engine: one idea turned into four formats, scheduled on a content calendar
- WhatsApp & Instagram sales assistant with a flow engine and RAG
- Lead panel and mini CRM wired to conversational channels
- AI-assisted B2B sales intelligence that tracks target companies' projects and tenders

**Also built:** a multi-tenant SaaS, native iOS and Flutter apps, and websites for brands and publishers.
